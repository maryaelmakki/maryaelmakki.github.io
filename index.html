Hello

I take concepts from script to finished sequence.

I'm Marya, a visual artist and AI creative. I build worlds, whether it's a film shaped from archival fragments, a character that never existed, or an illustration drawn line by line by hand.

Lover's Shell

Genre
Experimental documentary, art film
Visual style
Poetic, immersive, multi-screen cinematic

Drawing on archival research of Japanese ama free-divers and Gulf pearl divers, Lover's Shell reimagines a cross-cultural maritime history through a speculative early 20th-century love story between Mabrook, an African-born Gulf diver, and Paru, a Japanese ama diver.

Credit

What the brief was, what I did, what shipped.

I built the visual pipeline from archival source material: photographing and restoring Japanese diving archives in Photoshop, extending frames, removing figures to construct clean keyframes for image-to-video, and generating additional characters and period shots absent from the archive. I co-directed the edit, shot sequencing and music selection across both films.

AI-assisted proposal system · Confidential client

From days to minutes

A reusable system that turns supplied copy into on-brand proposals, built on approved layouts.

Manual workflow

Before

Built manually. Delivered in days.

Each proposal was rebuilt from a 40-page master, with layouts, formatting and imagery assembled by hand. The process was repetitive, time-intensive, and left room for inconsistencies between submissions.

Reusable system

After

Built systematically. Delivered in minutes.

Each proposal is assembled from approved layouts, with supplied content, formatting and imagery placed into a reusable structure. The process is faster, more consistent, and keeps every submission aligned with the brand.

  1. 01Content inProposal copy and project details are supplied.
  2. 02Layout mappingContent is mapped into the appropriate approved layouts.
  3. 03Visuals addedProject imagery and required assets are added.
  4. 04Designer reviewHierarchy, imagery and presentation are reviewed before delivery.

Handmade Rugs

Designed, drawn and tufted by hand.

Remember · تذكّر. The world does not need you to be exceptional for you to matter.

01

Inspiration

Created in response to the pressure to always become more successful, visible and exceptional. The work reminds us that worth already exists in who we are, what we survive, and the kindness we carry.

02

Concept

Tathakkar means remember. Repeated four times, it becomes an inner voice calling us back to the self that existed before comparison. Worth is something we already carry.

03

Visual approach

Monumental Arabic calligraphy turns a private affirmation into a public statement. Deep burgundy adds warmth and spiritual depth, while black geometry and cream lettering connect the piece to traditional woven forms.

From words to form

I created this 180 × 300 cm carpet at home using a tufting gun and a large wooden frame I built myself. Every letter and pattern was mapped and tufted by hand, slowly transforming a quiet sentence into a monumental physical reminder: you are already enough.

08

Level Concept

Spaces designed for play, with clues, routes and rules.

Client brief · Concept stage

Detective VR

Office murder case

A multi-user interactive VR environment. I delivered the character and level concept design and the level map.

The CEO case · Level map in 3D

Walk the level

The office plan rebuilt as a wireframe block model. Drag to orbit, use the controls to zoom or switch views, and play the route the player takes from the elevator. The rooms stay closed on purpose: this view shows space and flow, not what the player will find.

Drag to orbit

Interactive clue zoneNon-clue areaInteractive environmentNon-accessible areaSecurity cameraPlayer movement

Level structure

  1. The player spawns inside the elevator.
  2. The player steps into the reception area, the central hub for reaching every other room.
  3. The player must identify key clues.
  4. Piece together evidence.
  5. Present the final findings to resolve the case.

Design philosophy

A fully interactive crime scene where progress depends on how the player thinks: instinct, observation and deduction. Every room can be explored in any order, and physical evidence, digital evidence and testimonies combine to name the suspect.
